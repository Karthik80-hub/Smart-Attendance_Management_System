Privacy Policy

Smart Attendance Management System

Effective Date: May 30, 2025

1. Introduction

We at Smart Attendance Management System ("we", "our", or "us") are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our platform.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect and store the following user data:

  • Name and email address during user registration
  • Attendance records (timestamps and related metadata)
  • Facial data processed for verification (not stored as raw images unless explicitly configured)
  • Device IP address and browser info for session management

3. How We Use Your Data

Collected data is used only for:

  • Identifying users during login or attendance marking
  • Storing attendance logs
  • Improving service reliability and user experience
  • Sending registration or attendance confirmations

We do not sell or share user data with third parties.

4. Data Retention

Your data is stored securely on our AWS infrastructure. We retain:

  • Registration details until the account is deleted
  • Attendance logs for audit/reporting unless requested for removal

5. Data Security

We follow best practices including:

  • Encrypted HTTPS communication
  • SSL certificates (via Certbot)
  • Deployment using secured CI/CD pipelines on Jenkins
  • Controlled server access via SSH and IAM roles

6. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • View and export your attendance records
  • Request account deletion
  • Contact us to remove any personal data
